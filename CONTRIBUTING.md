# Feedback from specialists

We seek answers to whether the *specific* stated results are correct, already known, or useful. We are not inviting anyone to join a continuing project or asking for a free referee report. One correction, clear counterexample, or relevant old paper would already be valuable.

Please open an Issue naming a file and equation. Corrections and negative feedback are welcome; claim ownership of any subsequent investigation in accordance with your normal academic practices. There is no expectation of additional participation from the original initiator.
