# Origin and scope

This archive began as a curiosity-driven exploration of a difficult question in three-dimensional resistive magnetohydrodynamics (MHD). During the exploration, the work narrowed to a specific spectral question for a linearized MHD operator in toroidal geometry. This repository records the question, derivations, computational experiments, and literature leads reached along the way.

AI tools assisted with mathematical derivations, symbolic and numerical calculations, code, and literature exploration. The material is shared in the hope that some part may be useful to researchers working in related areas.

The archive does not claim to solve the original nonlinear 3D MHD global well-posedness problem, establish a new theorem, or demonstrate priority or novelty. The central eigenfunction question remains open in this archive.

Labels such as `VERIFIED` in some working notes refer to checks reported during the development process; they are not independent expert review. All mathematical claims should be assessed on their arguments and evidence.
