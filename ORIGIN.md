# Origin and scope

This project began with a simple curiosity: I wanted to explore a difficult question in three-dimensional resistive magnetohydrodynamics (MHD) and see where the investigation would lead. AI tools assisted with mathematical derivations, symbolic and numerical calculations, code, and literature exploration. The exploration developed into the specific spectral question and research materials collected here.

I am sharing these notes and computational materials in case some part may be useful to researchers working in related areas.

I do not claim that the original nonlinear 3D MHD global well-posedness problem has been solved, that a new theorem has been established, or that the results are novel. The central eigenfunction question remains open in this archive.

Labels such as `VERIFIED` in some working notes refer to checks reported during the development process; they are not independent expert review. All mathematical claims should be assessed on their arguments and evidence.
